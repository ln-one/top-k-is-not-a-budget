Diverse queries and evolving corpora make fixed Top-$L$ cutoffs difficult to transfer in hybrid retrieval for retrieval-augmented generation (RAG). Exact fusion avoids a fixed cutoff, but the reads needed to complete a fixed Top-$K$ remain query-dependent. We present DiBud (Direct Budgeting), which takes a read budget and returns the exact prefix certified within it. DiBud maintains bounds on unread contributions and prioritizes the channel blocking the next output position. The budget directly controls reads; the available evidence determines the output length. Replays on five query sets show a 1.06\%--5.95\% improvement in normalized certified-prefix area over balanced reading. Across five corpus snapshots, output lengths vary as the corpus changes, and every delivered prefix matches the corresponding complete reciprocal rank fusion ranking.
