# Working research narrative

1. Fixed result count is an execution contract, not a universally known user need.
2. Budgeted exact hybrid retrieval returns the longest certified complete-fusion prefix available under a resource constraint.
3. Dense and Sparse are heterogeneous resumable evidence sources and need not advance symmetrically.
4. Online source scheduling determines the attainable certified frontier.
5. Poison queries expose the tail behavior and limits of any finite-budget guarantee.
6. Logical-depth replay isolates algorithmic behavior; calibrated costs and live execution test operational validity.
7. The final contribution must be distinguished explicitly from TA/NRA, Upper-style scheduling, any-k enumeration, anytime IR, and EAHR.

## Outcome of the research narrative

Items 1--6 are empirically useful, but item 7 fails for the inexpensive
rank-only method: LARA-IN/SNRA cover the core and the current Dense source is
eager at the physical layer. This outline is retained as the investigated
hypothesis, not as a manuscript plan. Any future paper must start from a new
aggregate exact index or a separately calibrated semantic-risk task.
