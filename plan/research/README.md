# Comprehensive research workspace

budgeted exact hybrid-retrieval study. Files here are research records, not
manuscript prose. The final verified decision is in
`final-synthesis-zh.md`; `paper-path-decision.md` records the publication gate,
and `prior-art-map.md` preserves the source-level novelty audit. The study does
not recommend a standalone rank-only manuscript in its current form.
